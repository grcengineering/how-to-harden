# OpenAI Ads source coverage

Source-first discovery started 2026-11-05 before choosing controls. Scope: Ads Manager, account access, Ads API and OAuth integrations, conversion measurement, audience/feed data, audit and account spending controls. Ads has a distinct ad-account control plane, not an API Platform project. Baseline: no Ads guide or pack directory. Common identity/ownership evidence is coordinated with the OpenAI family ledger.

No tenants declared — Tier 0+1 only. Public source fetches and offline fixtures only; no credentials or live product requests/settings. Retrieval bodies and URL/time/status/SHA-256 are retained in the private API/Ads source cache. No live-validation or human-review status will be claimed.

## Retrieval record

All checks dated 2026-11-05. **34 successful direct developer-document fetches:** every one of the 32 Markdown pages in the official Ads index, its combined export, and `openapi.json`. The index itself was fetched by the parent lane. Bodies/time/status/SHA-256 are in the private `.hth-validation/openai-api-sources/` cache. The OpenAPI contract reports **2.3.0, 73 paths, 88 operations, two authentication schemes and one webhook**. These are inventory counts, not a completeness score.

Direct HTTP fetches to five Help Center pages returned anti-bot 403 responses. Successful official-page `web.open` retrievals supplied the actual text; the 403s are not absence findings. The guide cites **12 successfully opened Help Center pages**: Ads beta overview, Ads identity/access, SSO setup, tenant administration, Ads Manager plugin, HubSpot, GA4, Shopify, measurement partners, mobile partners, UI product feeds and Sponsored Agents. Additional account-setup/troubleshooting/conversion-measurement pages were investigated without originating separate claims. Public direct retrieval and successful fallback are distinct methods.
