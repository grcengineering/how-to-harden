Admitted controls are customer configuration or evidence procedures traceable to the first-party sources in the guides. The SSPM connection catalog establishes research scope only; its descriptions do not prove that a vendor can set or repair a particular configuration. OAuth token scopes, dashboard roles, and effective operator access were not validated against a live tenant.

## Admission and baseline limits

- HYPR's [public security compliance page](https://www.hypr.com/trust-center/security-compliance) and [security exhibit](https://www.hypr.com/trust-center/security-policy) describe assurance and vendor obligations, not how to configure customer access. Private trust-center documents were not accessed.
- Cisco's [Meraki trust page](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/about/corporate-strategy-office/acquisitions/meraki/trust.html) is not adopted as a checklist. Configuration is sourced from Dashboard administration procedures and API schemas, Umbrella's design guidance and Cisco DevNet; private assurance catalogs were not accessed.
- Microsoft's Copilot-specific sources are Microsoft Learn admin references, not platform assurance claims. The [Restricted SharePoint Search admin reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/restricted-sharepoint-search-admin-scripts) documents retirement and recommends Restricted Content Discovery going forward.
- [CIS benchmark catalog](https://www.cisecurity.org/cis-benchmarks) and CISA's [SCuBA reference](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2024-04/CSSO-SCuBA-Fact%20Sheet-FINAL_508c.pdf) identify relevant adjacent infrastructure and Microsoft 365 foundation coverage, not a whole-product baseline for these control planes. Product-specific CIS, STIG or SCuBA requirements were not established, and no NSA or ACSC mandate is implied.
- Cisco's [automatic Meraki/Umbrella integration procedure](https://documentation.meraki.com/Wireless/Operate_and_Maintain/How_Tos/Features_and_Integrations/Automatically_Integrating_Cisco_Umbrella_with_Meraki_Networks) says new automatic enablement was deprecated; do not use it as evidence for a new integration. Wired, wireless, VPN, SWG and endpoint-side enforcement remain independent of an organization dashboard policy.

These are scoped, AI-drafted guides. They do not claim a complete enumeration of all vendor capabilities, supported editions, API writes or device policy surfaces.
