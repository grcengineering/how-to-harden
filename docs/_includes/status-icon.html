{%- comment -%} Maturity mark — THE single source of every status icon on the site. The ladder is a matrix, not a line: three STAGES (drafted → reviewed → validated) each done by either AGENT (ai = artificial intelligence, ni = natural intelligence, i.e. a person), and the two are not exclusive. The mark encodes both axes so a reader can decode a badge they have never seen: STAGE = the glyph. draft mark (pencil) · eye (reviewed) · check (validated) AGENT = the spark. A four-point spark in the top-right means a machine did it. No spark means a person did it. Both agents share the SAME glyph, undistorted, at its native Feather-icon size — an AI mark and its NI twin differ by the spark and nothing else, and the empty top-right corner on an NI mark is exactly where its AI twin's spark sits. That is what makes "AI Validated + NI Validated" read as one thing done twice rather than two unrelated badges. Colour is the second channel: the caller's stylesheet paints AI in the LIGHTER shade and NI in the DARKER shade of the stage hue. GLYPH GEOMETRY (corrected 2026-08-20). Each glyph is Feather Icons' stock path data, drawn in its 24x24 viewBox with no group transform — Feather's own glyphs are already centred on that grid, which is exactly why the checkmark's raw bbox (x:[4,20] y:[6,17], centre (12, 11.5)) sits within half a pixel of the viewBox centre on its own. A prior version wrapped the glyph in `translate(-1.2 2.6) scale(0.8)` to shrink it and make room for the spark — that shift is what put the checkmark visibly off-centre inside the circular bubble it now renders in (reported live, 2026-08-20). The spark's own coordinates were authored against this UNTRANSFORMED glyph in the first place, so removing the transform is also what puts the spark back at the checkmark's actual top-right end rather than floating disconnected above it.
